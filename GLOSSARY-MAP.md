# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Fill the Mat](./GLOSSARY.md): martial-arts schools handling trial enquiries and bookings
- [Core](./docs/core/GLOSSARY.md): agents holding conversations for a business over many channels, independent of what it sells

## Relationships

- **Fill the Mat → Core**: Fill the Mat is an application of the core. A School is an Operator; a School Owner is Staff; the Assistant is an Agent; someone interested in a trial is a Counterpart, who may later be linked to a Contact; web chat, WhatsApp and Poppy are Channels.
- **Fill the Mat ↔ Core**: Conversation and Turn mean the same in both; Fill the Mat adds its lifecycle policy (30 days without a message, or the message limit).
